# Is She His Wife?: Reference

## Line of Work

Staging Charles Dickens's burletta _Is She His Wife?_ (1837). A comic burletta of domestic misunderstandings, jealousy, and social flirting at a country villa.

## Origin

Charles Dickens, _Is She His Wife?_, written in 1837, one of Shorter Fiction and Sketches in the house catalog (`csf_019`, [ISHW]). The text is in the public domain.
