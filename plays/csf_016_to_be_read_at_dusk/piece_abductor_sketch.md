# Piece: Abductor Sketch

## Taxonomy

The drawings of dream figures: a charcoal sketch piece.

## Owner

## Place

## Load Bearing

The physical evidence of the bride's premonition. Without this sketch, her husband and friends would not believe her dream warning when the real Italian Gentleman arrives.

## Apparent

A sheet of paper showing a charcoal drawing of a dark, enigmatic man's face, with a strange expression.

## Yearbook

- Yesterday, the sketch was drawn by the English Bride in her chamber after a terrifying dream.
- Today, the sketch is shown to her friends at the villa, who recognize the stranger when he arrives.
- Tomorrow, the sketch is left behind on the marble terrace, the only remaining trace of the vanished bride.
