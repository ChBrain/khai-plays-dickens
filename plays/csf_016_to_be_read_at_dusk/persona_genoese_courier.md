# Persona: Genoese Courier

## Taxonomy

The traveling storytellers: a knowledgeable Genoese traveler and narrator persona.

## Owner

- Project: [To Be Read at Dusk](play_csf_016_to_be_read_at_dusk.md)

## Projection

The Genoese Courier operating in his [Courier](position_courier.md) position, who tells the tragic story of the English Bride and her mysterious abductor in Genoa.

## Action

Recount the Genoese premonition to the group of couriers at Saint Bernard, describe the bride's terror, and carry the courier bag across Europe.

## Shadow

His theatrical delivery and interest in the supernatural can make his stories seem like tall tales to cynical listeners.

## Tell

He gestures dramatically with his pipe and looks off into the evening mist while speaking.
