# Pitch: Dusk Tale Pitch

## Taxonomy

The tuning of tone for the whole play: a production directive.

## Owner

## Tenor

A contrasting canvas: the windy, grey summits of Switzerland meeting the warm, marble villas of sunlit Genoa as twilight settles.

## Undertow

The slow, rhythmic puffing of pipes, the distant howling of pass winds, and the sudden, silent departure of a carriage in the dark.

## Nerve

The supernatural dread between the Bride's clear premonition and the real arrival of her dream abductor.

## Echo

A warning that some paths and meetings are written in the stars, and that the warnings of the dream world cannot be escaped.
