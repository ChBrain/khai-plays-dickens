# To Be Read at Dusk: Reference

## Line of Work

Staging Charles Dickens's shorter work _To Be Read at Dusk_ (1852). A dramatic tale of premonition, staging two romantic tragedies told by couriers on a mountain pass.

## Origin

Charles Dickens, _To Be Read at Dusk_, published in 1852, one of Shorter Fiction and Sketches in the house catalog (`csf_016`, [TBRD]). The text is in the public domain.
