# Position: Courier

## Taxonomy

The traveling couriers of Europe: a courier role.

## Owner

- Project: [To Be Read at Dusk](play_csf_016_to_be_read_at_dusk.md)

## Has

A courier bag, a route book, a travelers' pipe, and a collection of strange stories.

## Orders

Travel across European routes, guide travelers safely through passes, and share warnings and tales with fellow couriers.

## Loses

His way on the high mountain passes in bad weather, and his audience if his stories are too long.

## Drives

The circulation of news, stories, and warnings between distant cities and inns.
