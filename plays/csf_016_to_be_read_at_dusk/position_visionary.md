# Position: Visionary

## Taxonomy

The recipients of premonitions: a visionary role.

## Owner

- Project: [To Be Read at Dusk](play_csf_016_to_be_read_at_dusk.md)

## Has

A sketchpad, an active imagination, vivid dreams, and a premonitory warning.

## Orders

Record dreams of the future, sketch the faces of dream figures, and watch for their arrival in the real world.

## Loses

Her peace of mind when her dreams begin to manifest, and her freedom when she is carried away.

## Drives

The emotional and psychological stakes of the play through supernatural warnings.
