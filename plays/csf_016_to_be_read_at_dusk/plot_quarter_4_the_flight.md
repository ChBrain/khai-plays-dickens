# Plot: The Flight

## Owner

## Cue

A quiet, misty evening outside the villa terrace.

## Action

[Italian Gentleman](persona_italian_gentleman.md) executes his [Flight Plan](plan_flight.md), leading the helpless [English Bride](persona_english_bride.md) away, while [German Courier](persona_german_courier.md) recounts the warning of the [London House](place_london_house.md) to close the storytelling.

## Stage

The carriage departs in the dusk, leaving the bride's friends searching the gardens in vain, as the couriers on the pass sit in silence under the twilight sky.

## Tension

The finality of the bride's abduction and the ghostly warnings leave the characters and listeners in a state of quiet wonder.
