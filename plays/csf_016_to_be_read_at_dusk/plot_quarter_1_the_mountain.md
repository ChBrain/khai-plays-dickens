# Plot: The Mountain Summit

## Taxonomy

The fytte of the alpine gathering: a narrative plot.

## Owner

- Project: [To Be Read at Dusk](play_csf_016_to_be_read_at_dusk.md)

## Cue

A cold, windy evening on the Saint Bernard Pass.

## Action

The couriers rest at [Saint Bernard Pass](place_saint_bernard.md), placing the [Courier Bag](piece_courier_bag.md) on the bench as the [Genoese Courier](persona_genoese_courier.md) and [German Courier](persona_german_courier.md) in their [Courier](position_courier.md) positions begin to share tales of fate, guided by the [Dusk Tale Pitch](pitch_dusk_tale.md).

## Stage

[Genoese Courier](persona_genoese_courier.md) starts his narrative about Genoa, while the other couriers listen in the gathering mist on the pass.

## Tension

The transition from the cold reality of the mountain summit to the mysterious, supernatural atmosphere of the stories creates a quiet suspense.
