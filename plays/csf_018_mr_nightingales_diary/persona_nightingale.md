# Persona: Mr. Nightingale

## Taxonomy

The anxious hypochondriacs: a medicine-obsessed gentleman persona.

## Owner

- Project: [Mr. Nightingale's Diary](play_csf_018_mr_nightingales_diary.md)

## Projection

Mr. Nightingale operating in his [Hypochondriac](position_hypochondriac.md) position, who keeps a detailed diary of phantom diseases.

## Action

Record symptoms in his diary, consume multiple pills and mixtures, and reject Charley's marriage proposal.

## Shadow

His self-absorption comically isolates him from his daughter's wishes and makes him highly gullible.

## Tell

He repeatedly measures his pulse and inspects his tongue in the mirror.
