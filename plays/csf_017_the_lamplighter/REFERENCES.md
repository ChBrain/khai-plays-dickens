# The Lamplighter: Reference

## Line of Work

Staging Charles Dickens's farce _The Lamplighter_ (1838). A theatrical farce of errors, staging an eccentric astrologer, a niece, and a series of identity mix-ups.

## Origin

Charles Dickens, _The Lamplighter_, written in 1838, one of Shorter Fiction and Sketches in the house catalog (`csf_017`, [TL]). The text is in the public domain.
